Frequently Asked Questions

Getting started

What is Pheid?

Pheid is a group notification app. Every account has a handle. Share your @handle (or name@handle for a sub-channel), and anyone who enters it gets your push notifications. It's focused on one-way broadcasts so your messages always land as clear, actionable updates.

How do I subscribe to a channel?

Tap the subscribe button on the home screen and enter the handle shared by the channel's owner — @handle for their main channel, or name@handle for a sub-channel. You can also scan their QR code or tap a pheid.app/join link. You'll start receiving notifications immediately.

How do I create a channel?

Your main channel is created automatically with your handle. To create a sub-channel, tap the create button on the home screen, name it, and pick a short sub-code — subscribers join with subcode@yourhandle. Free accounts can create up to 3 sub-channels (your main @handle channel doesn't count); Pro accounts have no limit.

How do people find my channel?

Share your handle any way you like — text, email, poster, business card. The app also gives you a QR code to scan and a pheid.app/join link you can send. All three point to the same channel.

You can also make a channel public so people can find it by searching in Discover. Listing a channel is free on every plan.

Using Pheid

Can I schedule messages in advance?

Yes. When composing a message, tap the schedule button to send it later — one-time at a specific date and time, or recurring (daily, weekly, monthly). Schedules are timezone-aware and handle daylight saving transitions automatically.

Free accounts can have up to 3 pending scheduled messages at a time; Pro accounts have no limit. Scheduled messages can't include an attachment.

Does Pheid support dark mode?

Yes. Pheid follows your system theme by default, and you can force light or dark in Settings.

I'm not receiving notifications

Make sure notifications are enabled for Pheid in your device settings:

  • iPhone/iPad: Settings > Notifications > Pheid > Allow Notifications
  • Android: Settings > Apps > Pheid > Notifications

Some Android manufacturers (Samsung, Xiaomi, Huawei) have aggressive battery optimization that can delay notifications. Check your device's battery settings and exempt Pheid from optimization.

Managing a channel

Can someone help me manage my channel?

Yes. Invite co-admins from Manage admins in the channel's settings. Co-admins can send and schedule messages and manage subscribers. Each channel can have 1 co-admin on the free plan and up to 5 on Pro. An invite expires after 7 days if it isn't accepted.

Co-admins work under the channel owner's plan: an admin on a Pro owner's channel gets Pro limits, and the messages they send count toward the owner's monthly quota.

Can I send a message to only some subscribers?

Yes, with tags. Create tags such as "U12" or "Building B" and assign them to subscribers. Each channel can have 3 tags on the free plan and 20 on Pro, and assigning tags is free on both.

With Pro, you can then send a message only to subscribers with certain tags, right away or scheduled. A targeted message can reach up to 500 subscribers.

Can I attach a photo or PDF?

Yes, with Pro. Each message can carry one attachment: an image up to 5 MB or a PDF up to 10 MB. Pro includes 500 MB of attachment storage, shared across all your channels. Attachments can't be added to scheduled messages or templates.

Can I approve subscribers before they join?

Yes. Turn on Require approval for a channel and new subscribers wait until you or a co-admin approves them. Approval mode is free on every plan.

Can I save messages I send often?

Yes. Save them as templates and reuse them from the composer. The free plan includes 3 templates; Pro has no limit.

Can I export my subscriber list?

Yes, with Pro. Export a channel's subscribers as a CSV file with each subscriber's handle, subscription date, admin note and tags. You can export each channel once every 10 minutes.

Plans and billing

Is subscribing free?

Yes. Subscribing to channels is always free with no limits. Subscribers never pay; Pro is only for people who run channels.

What does Pro include?

Pro raises the limits of the free plan:

Pro also adds:

  • Targeted send: send to subscribers with certain tags, right away or scheduled, up to 500 recipients per message
  • Subscriber CSV export: handle, subscription date, admin note and tags, once per channel every 10 minutes
  • Attachments: one image (up to 5 MB) or PDF (up to 10 MB) per message, with 500 MB of storage

Free on every plan: unlimited subscribers per channel, a public listing in Discover, approval mode, and everything on the subscriber side.

On a channel with co-admins, limits follow the channel owner's plan. Pro is available as a monthly ($4.99) or yearly ($49.99) subscription.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your device's app store:

  • iPhone/iPad: Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Pheid
  • Android: Google Play Store > Menu > Subscriptions > Pheid

You'll keep Pro access until the end of your current billing period. After that, nothing is deleted: the free limits apply to anything new you create, and some Pro items are paused. See Terms of Service, section 5 for the details.

I need a refund

Refunds are processed by Apple or Google, not by us directly. Request a refund through:

  • Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Google Play: Google Play refund support

Account

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings in the App and tap "Delete Account." This permanently removes all your data including channels, subscriptions, and messages. This action cannot be undone.
