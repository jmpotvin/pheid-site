Pheid (pronounced /feed/)

Pick a handle, share it with your group, and everyone who subscribes gets your updates instantly. Built for coaches, landlords, teams, and communities who just need to get the word out.

How it works

Three steps from handle to notification

No group chat, no replies to manage, no phone numbers to collect.

01

Claim your handle

Your main channel lives at @handle. Add sub-channels for practice, parents or announcements.

02

Share it

Send the handle, a pheid.app/join link, or show the QR code. Anyone who enters it is subscribed.

03

Everyone gets the push

Write once. Every subscriber gets a notification, and the message stays in their inbox.

Features

Everything a one-way update needs

Subscribe with a handle

Enter @handle to follow someone's main channel, or name@handle for a sub-channel. Subscribers are in instantly.

One message, everyone hears it

Send once; every subscriber gets a push notification. Focused, one-way broadcasts keep your updates clear and easy to act on.

Schedule ahead

Queue one-off or recurring messages — timezone-aware, perfect for practice reminders, rent notices, and weekly updates.

Share by link or QR code

Every channel has a pheid.app/join link and a QR code. Put it in a text, an email or on a poster.

Pin what matters

Pin the message people keep asking about so it stays at the top of your channel.

Bookmarks for subscribers

Subscribers save the updates they'll need later, like a schedule or an address, and find them in one tap.

Co-admins

Invite helpers to send and schedule messages and manage subscribers on your channel, under your plan.

Targeted send Pro

Tag subscribers, then send only to the U12 team or the tenants of building B, right away or scheduled.

Photos and PDFs Pro

Attach a schedule, a flyer or a notice to a message you send: an image up to 5 MB or a PDF up to 10 MB.

Why “Pheid”?

One runner. One urgent message. Your group, notified instantly.

Named after Pheidippides, the ancient Greek messenger who ran from Marathon to Athens to deliver a single, urgent message. Pheid carries that same idea forward.
